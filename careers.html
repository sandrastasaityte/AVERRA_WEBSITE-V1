CANDIDATE INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

Some useful points to consider before expressing your interest.

Do I need international work experience?

Not necessarily. Requirements depend on the role. Relevant professional skills, experience, qualifications and the ability to work effectively with a remote team may be more important for a particular engagement.

Which career areas can I express interest in?

You can enquire about finance, accounting, data analytics, technology, engineering, architecture, business operations, customer support and related professional functions.

Are all opportunities remote?

AVERRA focuses on international remote staffing and outsourcing. The precise working arrangement depends on the individual client, role and agreed engagement terms.

Does registering guarantee employment?

No. Registering your interest does not guarantee an interview, placement or job offer. Opportunities depend on client demand, suitability and any applicable selection process.
