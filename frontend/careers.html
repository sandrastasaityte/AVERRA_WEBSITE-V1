Our approach

Talent should not be limited by geography

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd is a UK-based staffing and outsourcing business focused on connecting UK organisations with international professional talent.

Our approach helps businesses explore flexible staffing options while giving professionals a way to express interest in suitable remote roles.

Potential opportunities may include finance, accounting, administration, customer support, technology, engineering and other professional services.

Registering interest is not a job offer.

Opportunities depend on client demand, role requirements, experience, location, work authorisation and applicable engagement arrangements.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about careers at AVERRA

Find out what registering your interest means and what to expect.

Are these confirmed job vacancies?

No. These career areas describe the professional skills AVERRA is interested in. Specific opportunities depend on client requirements and business demand.

Can I register if I live in India?

Yes. AVERRA's business model focuses on connecting UK businesses with international professionals, including professionals based in India. Specific opportunities depend on the role and its requirements.

Does registering guarantee an interview or job?

No. Registering interest does not guarantee an interview, job offer or particular response time.

Is there a fee to register my interest?

This form does not ask for payment details. Be cautious of anyone claiming to represent AVERRA who requests unexpected payments or sensitive financial information.

How can I contact AVERRA about my registration?

Email info@averrastaffing.co.uk. Avoid sending sensitive identity or financial documents unless a legitimate process requires them and a secure method has been agreed.
