Our approach

Talent should not be limited by geography

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd is a UK-based staffing and outsourcing business focused on connecting UK organisations with international professional talent.

Our approach centres on helping businesses explore flexible staffing options while giving professionals a way to express interest in suitable remote roles.

Potential opportunities may include finance, accounting, administration, customer support, technology, engineering and other professional services.

Registering interest is not a job offer.

Opportunities depend on client demand, role requirements, experience, location, work authorisation and applicable engagement arrangements.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about careers at AVERRA

Find out what registering your interest means and what you can expect from the process.

Are these confirmed job vacancies?

No. The career areas on this page describe the kinds of professional skills AVERRA is interested in. They are not a list of confirmed vacancies. Specific opportunities depend on client requirements and business demand.

Can I register if I live in India?

Yes. AVERRA's business model focuses on connecting UK businesses with international professionals, including professionals based in India. Any specific opportunity will depend on the role and its requirements.

Does registering guarantee an interview or job?

No. Registering your interest does not guarantee an interview, a job offer or a particular response time. We may contact you if a relevant opportunity becomes available.

Is there a fee to register my interest?

This website form is intended to collect an expression of interest. AVERRA does not ask you to enter payment card or bank details into this form. Be cautious of anyone claiming to represent AVERRA who asks you for unexpected payments or sensitive financial information.

How can I contact AVERRA about my registration?

You can email info@averrastaffing.co.uk with your question. Avoid sending sensitive identity or financial documents unless a legitimate process requires them and an appropriate secure method has been agreed.
