Who We Are

A bridge between UK businesses and talent in India

Our purpose

To help UK businesses explore international skills while creating professional opportunities for talented people based in India.

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd is a UK outsourcing business that connects companies in the United Kingdom with skilled professionals based in India.

We believe businesses should be able to explore the talent they need without being limited to a single location. Our UK–India model aims to make international professional support more accessible to organisations looking for flexible ways to meet their needs.

Depending on the client's requirements, services may include finance-related support, administrative work, technical expertise, development and other professional functions suited to remote delivery.

We aim to establish clear expectations from the start, understand the work involved and help create productive working relationships between clients and professionals.
