About AVERRA

Connecting UK Businesses with Skilled Global Talent

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd connects UK businesses with skilled professionals based in India through remote staffing and outsourcing.

Who We Are

A Bridge Between the UK and India

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd is a UK-based outsourcing business that connects UK companies with skilled professionals based in India. We help businesses explore international talent while providing professionals in India with opportunities to contribute to global teams.

Our approach starts with understanding each client's needs, identifying suitable skills and supporting a clear working relationship between the business and its remote professionals.

We aim to help organisations build flexible teams, access specialist capabilities and make informed decisions about outsourcing.

Our Business Model

Outsourcing to India, Connecting the UK with India

We help UK businesses explore remote staffing solutions that connect business requirements with suitable international skills.

01

Understand the Business

We learn about the client's objectives, required skills, responsibilities, working arrangements and expectations.

02

03

Support Remote Collaboration

What We Do

Flexible Support for Growing Businesses

We help UK businesses explore access to remote professional capabilities through outsourcing arrangements. The scope of support depends on each client's needs and the services agreed.

  • Remote staffing and virtual team support
  • Finance and administrative support
  • Technical and development-related roles
  • Document and business process support
  • Access to skilled professionals based in India
