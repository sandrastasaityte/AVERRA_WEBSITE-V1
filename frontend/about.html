Who We Are

A practical connection between UK businesses and international talent

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd helps UK businesses explore flexible ways to access professional support from an international talent pool.

Businesses may need additional capacity for finance, administration, customer support, technology, engineering, design or other professional functions. We aim to understand those requirements and discuss suitable remote staffing and outsourcing arrangements.

Our approach is built around clear communication, defined responsibilities and a shared understanding of the work that needs to be delivered.

We recognise that every organisation is different. Our aim is to help businesses explore staffing options that fit their operational priorities, team structure and plans for growth.

Global talent. Shared goals. Connecting businesses with international professionals.

Our Business Model

Remote Staffing and Outsourcing

We help businesses explore professional support models based on their requirements, the scope of work and the agreed terms.

Remote Professional Support

Remote professionals can support agreed business activities without needing to work from the client's physical office. The appropriate arrangement depends on the role and contract.

  • Finance and accounting administration
  • Business and operational support
  • Technical and digital work
  • Customer and administrative services

Dedicated Staffing Arrangements

Where appropriate, businesses can discuss a more dedicated staffing arrangement with defined responsibilities, communication expectations and agreed commercial terms.

  • Role and skills requirements agreed in advance
  • Scope of work and responsibilities documented
  • Communication and reporting arrangements established
  • Service terms and payment arrangements agreed
Working Together

A Clear and Collaborative Approach

Our process is designed to help establish the requirements before a staffing arrangement begins.

1. Understand

We discuss your business, current challenges, required skills and the support you are looking for.

2. Define

We identify the proposed role, responsibilities, scope of work and relevant commercial expectations.

3. Coordinate

We discuss suitable staffing options and coordinate next steps in line with the agreed arrangement.

4. Communicate

We aim to maintain clear communication and review requirements as the business relationship develops.
