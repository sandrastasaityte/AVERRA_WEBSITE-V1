Who We Are

A bridge between UK businesses and talent in India

Our purpose

To help businesses access international skills and give professionals in India opportunities to contribute to organisations serving the UK market.

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd is a UK-based outsourcing business focused on connecting companies in the United Kingdom with skilled professionals based in India.

We believe businesses should be able to find the skills they need without being limited to a single location. By building connections across the UK and India, we aim to help organisations explore flexible ways to access professional support.

Depending on a client's requirements, this may include remote administrative support, finance-related services, technical expertise, development work and other professional functions suited to remote delivery.

Our approach centres on understanding business needs, identifying suitable talent and building working relationships around clear responsibilities, communication and agreed expectations.
