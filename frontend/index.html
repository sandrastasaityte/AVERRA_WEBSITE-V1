About AVERRA

Connecting UK businesses with talent in India

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd is an outsourcing business that connects UK companies with skilled professionals based in India. We help businesses access international talent while creating employment opportunities for professionals in India.

We aim to understand each client's requirements and identify appropriate professionals for remote business support. Responsibilities and working arrangements are agreed before an engagement begins.

  • Finance and administration support
  • Technology and development professionals
  • Remote staffing tailored to agreed requirements
  • Clear communication across UK–India teams
