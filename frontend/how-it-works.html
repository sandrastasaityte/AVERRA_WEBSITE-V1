Our approach

A staffing process built around your business

Every organisation has different priorities. You may need help with finance, accounting, administration, customer support, technology, engineering or another professional function.

AVERRA begins by understanding what your organisation needs to achieve. We then discuss the role, required skills, suitable staffing options and working arrangements.

The process

Six steps from discussion to delivery

The precise process depends on the role and service required. These stages provide a clear starting point.

01
Discover

Understand your business

We begin with a conversation about your organisation, current workload, business objectives and the tasks you need support with.

  • Your business activities and priorities.
  • Existing workload and resource gaps.
  • Expected outcomes and timing.
  • Budget considerations and constraints.
02
Define

Clarify the role and requirements

We establish the skills, responsibilities and practical requirements of the proposed position or service.

  • Role description and relevant experience.
  • Tasks, responsibilities and deliverables.
  • Working hours and time-zone overlap.
  • Communication, language and technical skills.
  • Reporting arrangements and systems.
03
Identify

Explore suitable professionals

Based on the agreed requirements, we explore potential staffing options and professionals with relevant experience. Depending on the role, this may include talent based in India.

  • Qualifications and relevant experience.
  • Role-specific and technical capabilities.
  • Communication and collaboration skills.
  • Availability and proposed working arrangements.

Suitability and availability are confirmed for each specific requirement.

04
Select

Review the proposed match

The proposed staffing option is assessed against your requirements. Where appropriate, this may include reviewing a profile, discussing experience and arranging an interview.

  • Review relevant experience and skills.
  • Discuss expectations and working style.
  • Confirm availability and potential start date.
  • Resolve outstanding questions before proceeding.
05
Agree

Confirm the service arrangements

Before work begins, the parties agree the scope, fees, payment terms, responsibilities and relevant contractual requirements.

  • Service scope and deliverables.
  • Fees, invoices and payment schedule.
  • Start date and working arrangements.
  • Confidentiality, data protection and security.
  • Review procedures and points of contact.
06
Collaborate

Onboard, communicate and review

Once the arrangements are confirmed, the parties plan onboarding, introductions, approved system access and communication. Progress can then be reviewed against the agreed scope and expectations.

  • Role introductions and task handovers.
  • Appropriate training and system access.
  • Regular communication and progress reviews.
  • Feedback and clarification of priorities.
  • Discussion of changing business needs.

International collaboration

We discuss the proposed arrangement with each prospective client before confirming the scope and next steps.

Getting started

Prepare for your first conversation

You do not need a complete job description to make an initial enquiry. A few details can help us understand what you are looking for.

1. Identify the work

Consider which tasks or responsibilities need support and what is difficult to manage internally.

2. Describe the skills

List essential experience, qualifications, technical skills and language requirements.

3. Consider working arrangements

Think about hours, time-zone overlap, communication channels and reporting requirements.

4. Establish priorities

Consider your preferred start date, budget, expected outputs and desired results.

5. Consider security

Identify relevant confidentiality, data protection, business system and access requirements.

6. Discuss the next steps

Use the initial conversation to clarify your requirement and consider suitable options.
