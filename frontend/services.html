Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Our Services

Learn more about AVERRA's UK–India remote staffing and outsourcing approach.

What does AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd do?

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd is an outsourcing business connecting UK companies with skilled professionals based in India. We help businesses explore remote staffing and outsourcing options for suitable business and technical functions.

Where are the professionals based?

Our business model focuses on professionals based in India who can provide remote support to UK businesses, subject to the role and working arrangement agreed.

Which types of roles can I discuss with AVERRA?

You can enquire about finance operations, administrative and document support, front-end development, technical roles and other suitable professional requirements. Availability and suitability depend on the specific role.

Can I discuss a specific business requirement?

Yes. Use our Talk to Us page to describe the work you need support with, the skills required and your preferred working arrangements.

How do I get started?

Contact AVERRA to discuss your requirements. We can then explore the scope of work, the potential arrangement and appropriate next steps.
