Important information

This page describes AVERRA's intended privacy approach and the types of processing that may occur. Before publication, the company should confirm that the statements reflect its actual operations, systems, suppliers, retention practices and legal obligations.

1. About this Privacy Policy

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd ("AVERRA", "we", "us" or "our") respects privacy and aims to handle personal information fairly, lawfully and transparently.

This policy applies to personal information processed in connection with our website, business enquiries, client relationships, staffing and outsourcing services, and recruitment-related activities.

It may be relevant to:

  • Website visitors and people contacting us.
  • Current and prospective business clients.
  • Business contacts, suppliers and advisers.
  • Candidates and international professionals.
  • Individuals involved in staffing engagements.

This policy should be read alongside any separate privacy notice, contract or data processing agreement that applies to a particular relationship or service.

2. Who we are

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd is a UK-based staffing and outsourcing business connecting UK businesses with skilled international professionals, including professionals based in India.

Depending on the particular activity, AVERRA may act as a data controller, a data processor, or in another role defined by applicable law and the relevant contractual arrangements. The applicable role depends on who determines the purposes and means of processing.

3. Information we may collect

The information processed depends on how you interact with AVERRA and the services involved. We aim to collect information that is relevant and proportionate to the purpose.

Business and client information

  • Name and professional job title.
  • Company or organisation details.
  • Business email address and telephone number.
  • Business correspondence and enquiry details.
  • Staffing requirements, role profiles and skills needs.
  • Proposal, contract and service-related information.
  • Billing and payment records where applicable.

Candidate and professional information

  • Name and contact details.
  • CV or résumé, employment history and work experience.
  • Professional skills, qualifications and certifications.
  • Availability and relevant role preferences.
  • Salary or compensation expectations where relevant.
  • Information provided during interviews or assessments.
  • References or eligibility information where lawfully required.
  • Records necessary to administer an agreed professional engagement.

Website and technical information

Depending on our hosting, security and website configuration, technical information may include IP addresses, browser and device details, operating system, access times, requested pages, security logs and limited usage information.

We will not assume that every type of technical or analytics data is collected. The actual information depends on the tools and settings in use.

4. Where information comes from

We may obtain information:

  • Directly from you when you contact us or complete a form.
  • When you email or telephone us.
  • When you provide a CV, application or professional profile.
  • From a client, referrer or professional contact.
  • From public professional sources where appropriate.
  • From service providers supporting an authorised business activity.

If information is obtained from another source, we will consider the applicable transparency requirements and provide information about the processing where required by law.

5. How we use personal information

Depending on the relationship and applicable law, we may process personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • Understand business staffing requirements.
  • Prepare proposals, quotations and service agreements.
  • Identify and assess potentially suitable professionals.
  • Support recruitment, introductions and staffing arrangements.
  • Deliver and administer agreed services.
  • Manage client, supplier and professional relationships.
  • Maintain business, invoicing and accounting records.
  • Protect systems, investigate suspected misuse and improve security.
  • Maintain and improve our website and services where permitted.
  • Meet legal obligations and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We will not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the original purpose without an appropriate lawful basis and any required transparency measures.

6. Lawful bases for processing

Where UK data protection law applies, we will identify an appropriate lawful basis for each relevant processing activity. Depending on the circumstances, this may be:

  • Contract: where processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you.
  • Legitimate interests: where we or another party have a legitimate interest, and that interest is not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is necessary to comply with a legal requirement.
  • Consent: where we rely on your valid consent for a specific purpose.
  • Other lawful grounds: where a different basis permitted by applicable law applies.

The lawful basis depends on the actual purpose and context. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Where special category data is involved, an additional condition under applicable data protection law may also be required.

7. Recruitment and candidate information

Where you apply for a role, provide a professional profile or are considered for a staffing opportunity, we may use relevant information to assess experience, skills, availability and suitability.

Where appropriate and lawful, candidate information may be shared with a client for consideration for a relevant role. We aim to limit the information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the recruitment or staffing purpose.

You should avoid sending unnecessary confidential information or sensitive personal data with a general enquiry. Please contact us if you need a suitable secure method for supplying documents.

If a client determines the purposes and means of its own recruitment processing, that client may have separate privacy responsibilities. The respective roles should be established for each arrangement.

8. Special category and sensitive information

We do not intend to collect unnecessary sensitive personal information through general website enquiries.

Special category data under UK data protection law can include information about health, racial or ethnic origin, religious or philosophical beliefs, political opinions, trade union membership, genetic or biometric data used for identification, and sex life or sexual orientation.

If such information is genuinely necessary for a particular lawful activity, we will assess the applicable legal conditions and safeguards before processing it.

9. Sharing personal information

AVERRA does not sell personal information. Where appropriate and lawful, information may be shared with:

  • Clients or prospective clients for relevant staffing purposes.
  • Professionals involved in an agreed service or engagement.
  • Website hosting, email and technology providers.
  • CRM, recruitment, document storage or security providers, where used.
  • Professional advisers and insurers where relevant.
  • Payment providers and financial institutions where necessary.
  • Regulators, courts, law enforcement or public authorities where required or legally permitted.

We seek to ensure that sharing is limited to an appropriate purpose and supported by the relevant legal basis, contractual terms and safeguards.

Service providers

Some providers may process personal information on our behalf. Where a provider acts as a processor, we will seek to put in place the written arrangements and security measures required by applicable law.

10. International data transfers

AVERRA's business model may involve UK businesses working with professionals based in India or other countries. As a result, certain personal information may need to be made available to people or service providers outside the UK.

An international transfer may be subject to specific UK data protection requirements. Where those requirements apply, we will assess the transfer and use an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism and safeguards. Depending on the circumstances, this may involve UK adequacy regulations, an applicable international data transfer agreement or addendum, or another lawful mechanism.

We will also consider whether access by overseas recipients is necessary, what information is involved, and what additional technical or organisational protections are appropriate.

The fact that a professional is based overseas does not, by itself, establish which transfer mechanism is required. The actual parties, data flows and applicable law must be assessed.

11. How long we keep information

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of legal requirements and legitimate business needs.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • The type and sensitivity of the information.
  • The purpose for which it was collected.
  • Whether a business or staffing relationship continues.
  • Applicable accounting, tax or other legal requirements.
  • Disputes, complaints or the need to establish or defend legal claims.

When information is no longer needed, we will take appropriate steps to delete it, anonymise it or securely dispose of it, subject to any legal requirement to retain it.

Specific retention periods should be established in AVERRA's internal retention schedule and applied to the systems actually used by the business.

12. Data security

We aim to use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks involved. Depending on the systems used, these may include access restrictions, account security, confidentiality obligations, secure storage, backups and appropriate supplier controls.

Access to personal information should be limited to people who need it for an authorised purpose.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will assess the incident and take action, including notifying the relevant authority or affected individuals where required by law.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies, depending on the features and third-party services that are enabled. These technologies may support essential website functions, security, performance or analytics.

Non-essential cookies and similar technologies will be used only in accordance with applicable consent requirements. If analytics, embedded content or other tracking tools are introduced, the relevant information and controls should be provided before those technologies are used where required.

The actual cookies in use should be verified against the deployed website. This policy does not confirm that a particular analytics or advertising service is installed.

14. Marketing communications

AVERRA may communicate with business contacts about its services where permitted by applicable law. The rules for electronic marketing can depend on the type of recipient, the communication channel and the circumstances in which contact details were obtained.

Where consent is required, we will obtain it before sending the relevant marketing communication. Where a different legal permission applies, we will comply with the conditions attached to that permission.

You can ask us to stop sending direct marketing communications at any time by contacting info@averrastaffing.co.uk .

15. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request erasure of information in certain circumstances.
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Object to certain processing, including direct marketing.
  • Request data portability where the legal conditions apply.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raise a concern about how your personal information is handled.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to conditions or exemptions. We may need to verify your identity before dealing with a request.

How to make a request

Email info@averrastaffing.co.uk with the details of your request. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information in your initial message.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If an extension or refusal is legally permitted, we will explain this where required.

16. Complaints

If you are concerned about how AVERRA has handled your personal information, please contact us first so that we can review your concern.

You may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Official guidance is available from the Information Commissioner's Office .

17. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended primarily for businesses, professional contacts, candidates and people involved in staffing or outsourcing activities. The website is not specifically directed at children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in circumstances where it should not have been collected, please contact us so that we can assess the matter.

18. External websites

Our website may link to websites or services operated by third parties. Those third parties have their own privacy practices and policies. AVERRA is not responsible for the operation or privacy practices of external sites it does not control.

We recommend reviewing the privacy information of an external service before providing personal information through it.

19. Automated decision-making

The use of automated tools in recruitment or staffing must be assessed according to how those tools operate and the effect they may have on individuals.

Where AVERRA uses automated processing that falls within applicable legal restrictions on solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, we will assess and implement the safeguards and information requirements that apply.

This policy should be updated if AVERRA introduces screening, profiling or decision-making tools that materially change how candidates or other individuals are assessed.

20. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, technology, business operations or legal obligations.

The current version will be published on this page with an updated revision date. Where required by law, we will provide additional information about material changes.
