01 About AVERRA

What does AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd do? +

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd helps UK businesses explore remote staffing and outsourcing solutions by connecting them with skilled professionals, including talent based in India.

Our aim is to help businesses identify appropriate support for their operational needs while agreeing clear responsibilities, communication arrangements and commercial terms.

What is remote staffing? +

Remote staffing enables a business to work with professionals who perform agreed duties remotely rather than from the client's physical premises.

Responsibilities, supervision, working arrangements, communication methods and commercial terms should be agreed before the engagement begins.

Why consider working with AVERRA? +

AVERRA focuses on understanding each client's requirements and exploring suitable global talent options. We aim to make the process clear, practical and aligned with agreed business objectives.

Suitability, availability, experience, costs and service arrangements are considered individually. Specific outcomes depend on the agreed engagement.

02 Services and roles

Which roles can AVERRA help businesses source? +

Depending on requirements and resource availability, roles may include:

  • Finance, bookkeeping and accounting support.
  • Administration and business operations.
  • Customer service and customer support.
  • Sales, marketing and business development support.
  • IT, software and technical support.
  • Engineering, architecture and other professional functions.
  • HR and recruitment support.

The role, required experience and scope of work are reviewed before confirming whether a suitable arrangement may be possible.

Can UK businesses work with professionals based in India? +

Yes. AVERRA's business model focuses on connecting UK businesses with global talent, including professionals based in India.

Working hours, communication, skills, supervision, information access and contractual responsibilities should be considered for each engagement.

Can a client request a dedicated remote professional? +

A dedicated staffing arrangement may be considered where the role, resource availability, workload and commercial terms are suitable.

The proposed allocation, duties, availability and reporting arrangements should be documented before work begins.

Can AVERRA support a business that needs multiple people? +

A business may enquire about support for multiple roles or a wider team. Requirements, delivery arrangements, resource availability and costs would need to be reviewed together.

Contact us with the roles, expected workload and preferred start dates so that your requirements can be discussed.

03 Process and onboarding

How does the staffing process work? +
  1. Discuss requirements: outline the role, skills, workload and business objectives.
  2. Review the proposal: consider the scope, fees, service arrangements and contractual terms.
  3. Identify a suitable resource: where applicable, review available professionals against the agreed requirements.
  4. Agree onboarding: confirm responsibilities, access, communication and the intended start date.

Steps and timescales depend on the nature of the engagement and should be confirmed directly with AVERRA.

How quickly can a professional start? +

Timescales depend on the role, resource availability, screening needs, client approvals and onboarding requirements.

A start date should be agreed once the requirements, availability and relevant contractual arrangements have been confirmed.

What information should a business provide before requesting support? +

To help assess your requirements, provide as much of the following as possible:

  • The role or business function requiring support.
  • Required skills, experience and qualifications.
  • Expected workload, hours and time-zone requirements.
  • Preferred start date and anticipated duration.
  • Relevant systems, training, security or compliance requirements.
  • Your preferred contact details and next steps.

Please do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.

04 Pricing and contracts

How much do AVERRA's services cost? +

Pricing depends on the role, required experience, scope of work, expected hours, service arrangements and duration.

Fees and payment terms should be set out in a written proposal before a client commits. Contact AVERRA to discuss your requirements and request a tailored quotation.

Is there a minimum contract period? +

Any minimum term, notice period or commitment depends on the service proposal and contract agreed between the parties.

Review the written terms carefully, including cancellation, notice, invoicing and any applicable minimum commitment, before signing.

How are payments and invoices handled? +

Invoicing frequency, payment deadlines, currency, taxes and any advance-payment arrangements should be stated in the relevant proposal or contract.

Refer to your agreed commercial terms for the payment schedule that applies to your engagement.

05 Data protection and security

How are data protection and confidentiality addressed? +

Data protection, confidentiality, access controls and contractual responsibilities should be considered for each engagement.

Before personal or confidential information is shared, the parties should establish which data-protection obligations apply and put appropriate contractual and operational safeguards in place.

Where personal data is accessed or transferred internationally, applicable UK data-protection requirements and any relevant international-transfer safeguards should be assessed.

Can a remote professional access our business systems? +

System access should be based on the duties being performed and the client's security requirements. Permissions, approved devices, authentication, training and confidentiality arrangements should be agreed before access is provided.

Businesses should provide only the access needed for the agreed work and avoid sharing personal passwords.

06 Careers and enquiries

How can I apply for opportunities with AVERRA? +

Interested professionals can visit our Careers page and contact AVERRA with information about their skills, experience and the type of work they are seeking.

Submitting an enquiry does not guarantee a role, interview or placement. Any available opportunity and application process must be confirmed directly.

Does AVERRA guarantee employment or placement? +

No. An enquiry or application does not guarantee employment, a client assignment, an interview or placement. Any opportunity depends on available roles, suitability, client requirements and the relevant selection process.

How can my business contact AVERRA? +

Visit our Let's Talk page and describe your staffing requirements, preferred skills, expected workload and desired timescales.

You can also email info@averrastaffing.co.uk. AVERRA can review your enquiry and discuss whether a suitable arrangement may be possible.
