What does AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd do?

AVERRA is an outsourcing business that connects UK companies with skilled professionals based in India. We aim to help businesses access remote talent for suitable business functions and projects.

Where are AVERRA's professionals based?

Our service model focuses on professionals based in India supporting businesses in the United Kingdom remotely. The practical arrangement is discussed and agreed for each engagement.

What types of roles can AVERRA help with?

Depending on the requirement and candidate availability, potential role areas include:

  • Finance and accounting
  • Administration and business support
  • Customer support
  • Sales and marketing
  • IT and technology
  • Engineering and architecture

Tell us about the responsibilities, required skills and experience so we can assess the request.

Can a professional work remotely for our UK business?

Remote working is central to AVERRA's model. Working hours, communication, equipment, supervision, system access and deliverables should be agreed for each engagement.

Can we request a particular skill set or level of experience?

Yes. Describe the role, essential skills, relevant experience, software or tools, working hours and expected outcomes. AVERRA can then assess the requirement and discuss whether it can be supported.

How does the process work?

The process generally follows these steps:

  • Discuss your business needs and objectives.
  • Define the role, skills, responsibilities and working arrangements.
  • Review suitable profiles and discuss selection.
  • Agree scope, fees, responsibilities and contract terms in writing.
  • Plan onboarding and access before work begins.

The exact process depends on the requirements of the engagement.

How long does it take to find a suitable professional?

Timing depends on the role, skills required, candidate availability and selection process. After learning about your requirements, we can discuss a realistic timeline.

How does onboarding work?

Onboarding should cover responsibilities, objectives, working hours, communication channels, tools, reporting, training and access permissions. The steps are agreed for each engagement.

Who manages day-to-day work?

Responsibilities should be agreed before the engagement starts, including who assigns tasks, sets priorities, reviews work, communicates feedback and handles performance questions.

How much do AVERRA's services cost?

Costs depend on the role, experience, scope of work and agreed working arrangement. Contact us with your requirements to discuss a proposal. Pricing should be confirmed in writing.

Are there long-term contracts?

Contract length and terms depend on the engagement. The scope, fees, payment terms, notice arrangements and responsibilities should be set out in the written agreement before work begins.

How are payment terms agreed?

Payment timing and invoicing arrangements should be agreed in the proposal or contract before services start. Please refer to the written terms for the particular engagement.

How is confidential information protected?

Confidentiality obligations, system access, secure working practices and information-handling responsibilities should be addressed before work starts. Businesses should provide access only to information and systems necessary for the agreed duties.

Can professionals access our systems and customer data?

Any system or data access must be assessed and agreed for the engagement. Access should be limited to what is necessary, with appropriate permissions and security controls. Applicable data protection requirements should be reviewed before access is granted.

How are data protection responsibilities handled?

Responsibilities depend on the services and parties involved. The relevant parties should establish their roles, lawful basis where applicable, security measures, data-processing terms and any international data-transfer requirements before personal data is accessed or transferred.

How do I apply for a job?

Visit our Careers or Jobs page and follow the application instructions for the relevant opportunity. If no suitable vacancy is listed, you can email us to ask about future opportunities.

Does sending my CV guarantee a job?

No. Applications are considered against relevant role requirements and candidate availability. Sending a CV does not guarantee an interview, placement or job offer.

Can I contact AVERRA if I do not see a suitable vacancy?

Yes. Email info@averrastaffing.co.uk with a brief introduction and the type of role you are interested in. Avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is requested through an appropriate channel.

How can I contact AVERRA about my business requirements?

Email info@averrastaffing.co.uk or visit our Let's Talk page. Include the type of support you need, key responsibilities, expected start date and any essential skills.
