Frequently asked questions

Industries and outsourcing explained

Answers to common questions about exploring India-based professional support for a UK business.

Which industries does AVERRA support?

Our areas of interest include finance and accounting, technology, administration, architecture and design, customer support, data and reporting, business operations, HR administration and research. The suitability of each requirement depends on the skills needed and the arrangement agreed.

Can a small UK business use your services?

Outsourcing may be worth exploring for businesses of different sizes. We can discuss your workload, budget, required skills and whether an external support arrangement is appropriate for your needs.

Are the professionals based in the UK or India?

AVERRA's business model focuses on connecting UK businesses with skilled professionals based in India. The location, working arrangements and responsibilities for any engagement should be agreed in advance.

Can we request a specific skill or role?

Yes. You can tell us the role, experience, technical skills, expected responsibilities and working requirements. Any proposed match will depend on availability and suitability.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the role, required expertise, scope of work, hours and proposed engagement structure. Contact us to discuss your requirements before making a decision.

How do you approach confidential business information?

Data protection and confidentiality requirements should be considered before work begins. The appropriate contractual terms, system permissions, access controls and international data-transfer arrangements depend on the information involved and the parties handling it.

What if our industry is not listed?

Contact us to explain your requirements. The listed areas are examples of potential support functions, rather than a guarantee that every role or specialism is currently available.
