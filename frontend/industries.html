Frequently asked questions

Industries and outsourcing explained

Learn more about exploring India-based professional support for a UK business.

Which industries does AVERRA support?

Our areas of interest include finance and accounting, technology, administration, architecture and design, customer support, data and reporting, business operations, HR administration and research. Suitability depends on the skills needed and the arrangement agreed.

Can a small UK business use your services?

Outsourcing may be worth exploring for businesses of different sizes. We can discuss your workload, budget, required skills and whether an external support arrangement is appropriate.

Are the professionals based in the UK or India?

AVERRA's business model focuses on connecting UK businesses with skilled professionals based in India. Location, working arrangements and responsibilities should be agreed before an engagement begins.

Can we request a specific skill or role?

Yes. You can describe the role, experience, technical skills, responsibilities and working requirements. Any proposed match depends on availability and suitability.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the role, required expertise, scope of work, hours and proposed engagement structure. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

How do you approach confidential business information?

Confidentiality and data protection requirements should be considered before work begins. Appropriate contractual terms, system permissions, access controls and international data-transfer arrangements depend on the information involved and the parties handling it.

What if our industry is not listed?

Contact us to explain your requirements. The listed areas are examples of potential support functions, rather than a guarantee that every role or specialism is currently available.
