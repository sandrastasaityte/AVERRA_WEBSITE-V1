THE DETAILS

How each stage works.

The precise steps depend on the engagement. These stages help establish a shared understanding before the work begins.

01
STEP ONE · DISCOVER

Understand your requirements.

We begin with a conversation about your organisation, business priorities and the support you are seeking. The aim is to understand the work before exploring a possible solution.

What we may discuss

  • Your business objectives and current challenges.
  • Role responsibilities and expected deliverables.
  • Required skills, experience and qualifications.
  • Working hours and communication preferences.
  • Expected start date and duration of support.
02
STEP TWO · DEFINE

Explore a suitable staffing model.

With a clearer understanding of your needs, we can discuss possible ways to structure the engagement. The appropriate model depends on the work, availability and your preferred operating arrangements.

Potential arrangements

  • Support from one remote professional.
  • Several professionals across different roles.
  • A dedicated remote team, where suitable.
  • Outsourcing for an agreed business function.
  • A defined scope of work with agreed deliverables.

Any proposed model remains subject to resource availability and agreement between the relevant parties.

03
STEP THREE · IDENTIFY

Identify and review potential professionals.

Once the requirements and proposed arrangements are clear, potential professionals may be identified for consideration where candidate sourcing forms part of the service.

Areas to consider

  • Relevant professional experience.
  • Technical and role-specific skills.
  • Qualifications or certifications, where required.
  • Communication skills and availability.
  • Screening and assessment steps agreed for the role.
04
STEP FOUR · SELECT

Review, interview and select.

Where introductions or interviews form part of the engagement, you can assess the relevant experience and suitability of potential professionals against your agreed requirements.

Selection considerations

  • Review relevant candidate information.
  • Arrange interviews where appropriate.
  • Assess skills and experience against the role.
  • Discuss responsibilities and availability.
  • Confirm the preferred way forward.
05
STEP FIVE · AGREE

Confirm the engagement and onboarding.

Before work begins, the relevant parties should understand the scope, responsibilities, fees, payment arrangements and applicable contractual terms. Practical onboarding requirements can then be coordinated.

Items to confirm

  • Scope of services and responsibilities.
  • Fees, invoices and payment terms.
  • Duration, notice and change arrangements.
  • Confidentiality and data protection requirements.
  • System access, contacts and onboarding steps.
06
STEP SIX · COLLABORATE

Establish communication and working practices.

Once work begins, the engagement follows the agreed responsibilities, reporting arrangements and communication methods. Any proposed changes should be discussed with the relevant parties.

Ongoing considerations

  • Clear communication channels and points of contact.
  • Defined responsibilities and expectations.
  • Review of agreed tasks and deliverables.
  • Appropriate escalation of issues.
  • Discussion of future staffing requirements.
