What shapes your quote

A proposal based on the work you need done.

The cost of staffing depends on the professional capabilities required and how the engagement will operate. We begin by understanding the practical requirements rather than assuming every position needs the same arrangement.

  • Role seniority and professional experience

Pricing questions

Frequently asked questions.

Here are some useful starting points when considering staffing or outsourcing with AVERRA.

Do you publish fixed prices?

No universal price is published on this page. Fees depend on the role, experience, working arrangements and agreed scope. Contact AVERRA to discuss your requirement and request a tailored proposal.

How do I request a quote?

Use our contact page to tell us about your business, the work involved and the professional support you need. We can discuss the requirement and potential next steps.

Can I request several professionals?

Yes, you can discuss a team-based requirement. The proposal will depend on the number of roles, the combination of skills needed and the proposed working arrangements.

Do you support businesses outsourcing to India?

AVERRA's business model focuses on connecting UK organisations with international talent, particularly professionals based in India. Specific role availability and engagement arrangements must be discussed and confirmed.

Are onboarding and additional costs included?

Any onboarding fees, additional services, expenses or other charges should be identified in the proposal where applicable. Review the written commercial terms before agreeing to an engagement.

What payment terms will apply?

Payment terms depend on the agreed commercial arrangement. The proposal and contract should specify the applicable fees, invoice schedule, payment due dates and any relevant deposits or advance payments.

Can the arrangement change as my business grows?

You can discuss changes to team size, responsibilities or support requirements. Any change to scope, pricing or contractual terms should be agreed before it takes effect.

Does requesting a quote commit me to hiring?

Requesting an initial discussion or quote does not itself create an engagement. Any commitment will depend on the terms agreed and the relevant contract or written confirmation.
