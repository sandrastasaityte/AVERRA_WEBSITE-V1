WHO WE ARE

A smarter conversation about building your team

Finding the right people is essential to business growth. However, recruitment pressures, changing workloads and the need for specialist skills can make workforce planning challenging.

AVERRA Staffing Solutions Ltd is a UK-based staffing and outsourcing business connecting UK organisations with international professional capabilities, including suitable talent based in India.

Our approach starts with your business rather than a predefined solution. We seek to understand the role, the work involved, the skills required and the outcomes you want to achieve.

Depending on your requirements, we can explore individual remote professionals, ongoing operational support or dedicated remote teams. The objective is to establish a working arrangement with clearly defined expectations and responsibilities.

UK + INDIA + GLOBAL TALENT

Connecting UK businesses with international talent

Businesses do not always need to source every capability from the local labour market. International staffing can provide another option when the role, working arrangements and business requirements are suitable.

AVERRA focuses on connecting UK organisations with international professionals, including talent based in India. Our aim is to make the relationship between business requirements and professional capabilities clear from the beginning.

Working across countries requires thoughtful planning. Responsibilities, working hours, communication, access to business systems, confidentiality and data protection should all be considered as part of the arrangement.

The appropriate commercial and operational structure will depend on the engagement. We aim to establish clear expectations and documented terms suited to the work involved.
